Primary Research Focus

🦠

Viral Bioinformatics

In silico discovery of host-derived microRNAs targeting human and agricultural viral genomes (RSV, Influenza, Mumps, MCMV).

🧬

NGS & Transcriptomics

High-throughput RNA-Seq, Whole Exome, and smRNA-seq data processing pipelines tailored for polyploid genomes and complex traits.

⚡

Epigenomics & Small RNA

Investigating mobile small RNAs as key drivers of systemic adaptation, environmental stress response, and epigenomic remodeling.
