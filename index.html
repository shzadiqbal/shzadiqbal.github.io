About

I am a molecular biologist and bioinformatician currently serving as Lecturer in Biochemistry at the University of Okara, Pakistan.

My research focuses on understanding host–pathogen interactions at the molecular and genomic level, with particular emphasis on viral bioinformatics, transcriptomics, and Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS).
