Academic Profile

Biochemist & Computational Geneticist

I serve as a faculty researcher and educator at the University of Okara, bridging state-of-the-art high-throughput sequencing analysis with molecular virology and biochemical diagnostics.

My work centers on mapping viral dynamics, identifying host-derived microRNAs for therapeutic gene silencing, and building resilient bioinformatics pipelines for high-dimensional genomic datasets.

Lab Vision

Research Areas & Methodologies

Leveraging wet-lab molecular techniques alongside high-performance computing clusters to solve bio-health challenges.

🦠

Viral Bioinformatics

Computational profiling of animal and plant viral pathogens to identify novel antiviral microRNA target sites and genomic escape variants.

  • Influenza, RSV & Mumps miRNA target analysis
  • Sugarcane Bacilliform & Maize Chlorotic Mottle Virus studies
📊

High-Throughput NGS Analysis

End-to-end processing of Whole Exome (WES), Whole Genome (WGS), and Transcriptomic datasets for disease risk biomarker discovery.

  • Polyploid crop genome RNA-seq (Sugarcane 72+ samples)
  • Human GWAS & adipose eQTL colocalization analysis
⚡

Small RNA & Gene Silencing

Exploring mobile small RNAs as mediators of systemic stress adaptation, epigenomic remodeling, and crop protection via RNAi.

  • PMRi & dsRNA ingestion therapies
  • Cross-kingdom miRNA regulation
