About the Principal Investigator

I am a molecular biologist and bioinformatician currently serving as Lecturer in Biochemistry at the University of Okara, Pakistan.

My research group focuses on understanding host–pathogen interactions at the molecular and genomic level, with particular emphasis on viral bioinformatics, transcriptomics, and Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) pipeline development.

Research Focus & Key Domains

My research integrates experimental molecular biology with computational genomics to decipher host–pathogen interactions, viral dynamics, and gene expression regulation.

01

Genomics (Host–Pathogen Interactions)

Deciphering molecular crosstalk between host genomes and invading pathogens using high-throughput sequencing and genomic colocalization analysis.

02

Molecular Virology

Computational prediction and molecular docking of microRNAs against plant and human viruses (RSV, Influenza A/C, Mumps, MCMV, Sugarcane Bacilliform Virus).

03

NGS & Transcriptomics

Custom pipeline creation for differential expression analysis (DESeq2, EdgeR), variant calling, and transcriptome profiling in complex polyploid crop genomes.

04

Host-Derived microRNA Prediction

Identifying cross-kingdom and host-encoded microRNAs capable of silencing viral genomes via ingestion or target-site transcript regulation.
