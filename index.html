About

I am a molecular biologist and bioinformatician currently serving as Lecturer in Biochemistry at the University of Okara, Pakistan. I combine wet-lab molecular biology with advanced computational analysis.

My work focuses on understanding host–pathogen interactions at the molecular and genomic level, with particular emphasis on viral bioinformatics and transcriptomic responses.
